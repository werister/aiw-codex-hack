# Data sources and redistribution

Checked on 31 October 2026. This inventory records the original input files, their exact byte sizes, and the release decision. Removed paths are listed for auditability; they are not download links. A public URL alone is not a redistribution licence. The proposed code licence does not replace third-party data terms.

## Restricted label source

DFLD does not grant an open redistribution licence in its [copyright notice](https://www.dfld.de/DFLDimprint.php?L=G). It requires written consent for uses beyond applicable copyright exceptions and restricts downloaded copies to private, noncommercial use. The extracted direction/status tables, row-level prediction/outcome copies, and fitted estimator weights are excluded from the clean source tree. The release uses a weather-rule demonstration instead. Restoring these materials for redistribution requires written permission or an independent rights assessment. Project-authored aggregate measurements remain as a historical evaluation record; they do not grant rights to the underlying labels.

The historical walk-forward results depend on those labels. Removing them prevents a fresh clone from independently retraining or recomputing the historical evaluation. The weather-rule demonstration runs with METAR-derived issue state; that state is not a recorded operating direction. It is a real computation from licensed weather, not a learned model, and the historical trained-model accuracy must not be attributed to it. Do not relabel synthetic or rule-derived targets as independent ground truth.

## Sources that permit redistribution

- **IEM:** its [data policy](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/disclaimer.php) places website material in the public domain. Credit Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University. These files normalize EDDF wind reports; they are not certified flight-operation data.
- **NOAA/NWS:** its [data policy](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer) permits lawful use of public-domain material unless otherwise marked. Do not imply NOAA endorsement or present modified data as official government output. Live EDDF METAR/TAF snapshots are normalized API responses.
- **Open-Meteo:** API data are [CC BY 4.0](https://open-meteo.com/en/licence). Credit Open-Meteo, link the [licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and identify normalization/derived features as changes. Place a linked attribution beside displays of its weather. The licence page also identifies upstream DWD, ECMWF and NOAA inputs. The [free API service](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) is restricted to noncommercial use and request limits; the data licence and API access terms are separate. Airport/airline business use needs an appropriate service agreement.
- **DWD CDC:** the [CDC terms](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/Nutzungsbedingungen_German.pdf), dated May 2024, specify CC BY 4.0. Credit Deutscher Wetterdienst, Climate Data Center; values here have been converted to UTC, normalized for missing sentinels, and converted from m/s to knots where named. These terms do not automatically cover DWD books or PDFs.

No provider logo or third-party reference photograph is included. This is independent of Fraport and airlines and is a decision-support demonstration, not for operational use.

## Removed research archive

The entire `weather database/` archive is excluded. It is not required by the serving model or dashboard. This also avoids redistributing mixed publication licences, copied publisher pages, extracted full texts, and optional US delay research as if they were MIT-licensed project data. The table records every original tracked file, including supporting metadata. Where a paper has a clear open licence, removal is a scope/size choice rather than a claim that reuse is forbidden. Unverified terms stay unverified.

BTS records cover US carrier/airport/month delay causes, not Frankfurt, individual flight rotations, hourly demand, or airport operating direction. Removing them loses an optional research dashboard; it does not remove a model input. Public-sector origin alone is not used here as proof of rights to every third-party element in downloaded web pages or reports.

## Rebuilding inputs without republishing restricted data

```sh
python -m ml.fetch --since 2024-01-01 --sources metar previous
```

This keyless weather downloader retrieves licensed weather sources and records provenance. It does not establish historical operational labels. See `python -m ml.fetch --help` for source selection and end dates. The DFLD extractor is source code only: use it only after establishing permission for the intended access, training and redistribution. No release workflow should automatically restore excluded DFLD tables.

A replacement operational-label dataset must have explicit training and redistribution rights, a recorded-state definition, UTC timestamps, availability times or an honest availability assumption, and missing/change-hour handling. A small synthetic fixture can exercise interfaces but cannot reproduce historical performance.

## Technical findings preserved from the source review

- Archived `_previous_dayN` values are lead-offset products, not a historical publication log. The six-hour publication buffer is an assumption; `best_match` lacks a rowwise source model/run identity. Historical retrieval time does not prove issue-time availability.
- Stable-hour operating-direction labels omit within-hour transitions and ambiguous daylight-saving hours. State accuracy alone cannot measure change-event detection. DFLD status is a public proxy, not a certified movement or weather-closure log.
- Historical runway-18 displayed non-use and rule-derived weather unavailability are distinct targets. Non-use can reflect demand, night restrictions or maintenance; it is not a validated wind-closure label.
- A wind-bias diagnostic from a separate ICON Global support archive does not establish the same bias for the serving `best_match` inputs. Any correction needs its own chronological training/validation comparison.
- Certainty is a probability-margin score, not a calibrated probability of joint correctness. The next-change direction probability is not an event probability; its heuristic timing window has no established coverage guarantee.
- Optional literature and US delay aggregates cannot support Frankfurt flight-impact counts, minutes of planning buffer, cost savings, or causal claims without local linked data and a validated action policy.
