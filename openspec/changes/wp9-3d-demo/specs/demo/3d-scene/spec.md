# Spec Delta

## Purpose
Show a non-aviation audience in a few seconds what a change of operating direction at FRA looks like.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Clear direction switch
The scene SHALL show the four FRA runways, the wind, and aircraft using OD 25, then switching to OD 07 when the wind turns.

#### Scenario: Jury sees the switch
- **WHEN** the scene plays
- **THEN** the aircraft flow reverses direction as the wind arrow turns east
