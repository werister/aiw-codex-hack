# Data Sources for the Frankfurt Airport Forecast

> Superseded. This is the early research from the morning of 30 Oct. The data we use is in `data/README.md` and `DATA_AND_DECISIONS.md`. Some statements below are out of date: DWD station 01420 is in the 10-minute archive, and DFLD gives recorded operating directions.

**Research date:** 30 October 2026  
**Target:** Hourly forecasts up to 48 hours ahead for Runway 18 availability and airport operating direction (25/07).

## Recommended sources

| Need | Source | What it provides | Use and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Historical observed wind | [DWD Climate Data Center (CDC), hourly wind](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/climate/hourly/wind/) | Hourly mean wind speed and direction from German stations. The DWD historical archive lists station **01420 (Frankfurt/Main)** from 1967–2025. | Strong free baseline for historical features. Confirm the station's exact location relative to the runways, inspect quality flags and station/instrument changes, and check current/recent data coverage before use. Hourly means will not capture all gust or short-lived crosswind thresholds. See the [dataset description](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/climate/hourly/wind/DESCRIPTION_obsgermany_climate_hourly_wind_en.pdf) and [historical files](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/climate/hourly/wind/historical/). |
| Higher-frequency observed wind | [DWD CDC, 10-minute wind archive](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/climate/10_minutes/wind/historical/) | Ten-minute wind observations for stations listed in the archive. | Station 01420 was not found in the archive listing checked for this research. Verify a suitable airport-area station and its measurement fields before relying on this source for gusts or runway-level conditions. |
| Live aviation observations and short-term forecast | [NOAA Aviation Weather Center Data API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) | Worldwide METAR and TAF products; query EDDF for current aviation observations and terminal forecasts. | Good live source and useful for a short backtest. The AWC documentation says its display has recent observations and directs older archive requests to NCEI; it is not a long-term training archive by itself. Keep the raw report and its observation/issue time. |
| 48-hour forecast wind | [DWD ICON-D2 Open Data](https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/nwp/icon-d2/) | High-resolution regional numerical forecasts covering Germany and surroundings. The model documentation describes runs every three hours with a 48-hour forecast range. | Directly fits the challenge horizon. Use wind direction and speed at the closest grid cells to the runways, and preserve model initialization time so each backtest uses only forecasts that existed at that time. DWD's [ICON-D2 documentation](https://www.dwd.de/SharedDocs/downloads/DE/modelldokumentationen/nwv/icon_d2/icon_d2_dbbeschr_aktuell.pdf?nn=344870&view=nasPublication) describes model cycles and forecast range. |
| Current operating direction and benchmark forecast | [Umwelt- und Nachbarschaftshaus (UNH) current direction and forecast](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/anwendungen-service/aktuelle-betriebsrichtung-und-prognose/) | Current direction attributed to DFS and a forecast for upcoming days. UNH explains that its forecast uses GFS weather forecasts and expert meteorological assessment; it presents forecasts in eight-hour periods. | Useful for checking the current label and comparing your direction forecast. This is an existing forecast, not independent ground truth for a historical test. The page does not document a bulk historical data API. |
| Movement and runway context | [UNH flight movement monitoring](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/fluglaermmonitoring/monitoring-der-flugbewegungen/) and [INAA monitoring tool](https://inaa.umwelthaus.org/) | Public monitoring views of flight movements and airport noise. UNH reports describe movement data based on Fraport/DFS radar information. | Potential way to inspect movements and runway use, but public machine-readable historical coverage is unclear. Confirm data permissions and export options before using it as a training-label feed. |
| Operational rules and direction definitions | [Fraport runway system](https://www.fraport.com/en/sustainability/dialog-with-neighbors/noise-and-air/flight-operations/runway-system-and-operating-hours.html), [Fraport operating direction](https://www.fraport.com/en/sustainability/dialog-with-neighbors/noise-and-air/flight-operations/operation-direction.html) | Official descriptions of the runway layout and operational direction. | Use these to interpret runway/operating-direction labels. The hackathon PDF's thresholds should remain the project baseline; operational practice can include other factors. |

## Recommended MVP data pipeline

1. Download DWD hourly historical wind for station 01420 and inspect station metadata, location, and quality flags.
2. Retrieve and archive ICON-D2 forecast runs. For each historical forecast origin, select only the run that would have been available at that time; extract wind features for the airport and relevant runway approaches.
3. Ingest live EDDF METAR/TAF reports for current conditions and short-term checks. Store issue/observation timestamps and raw text.
4. Compute runway tailwind and crosswind components from wind vectors and runway headings. Apply the hackathon brief's thresholds to create a **rule-derived Runway 18 usability baseline**.
5. For operating-direction prediction, compare model output with the current DFS-attributed direction from UNH. For historical supervised training and robust evaluation, first obtain or establish a permitted, time-stamped historical direction label set.

## Ground-truth gap

Weather data are readily available; dependable historical operating-direction and Runway 18 usage labels are the bottleneck. The UNH page provides current direction and forecasts, and monitoring pages expose flight information, but the sources checked do not document a simple bulk historical API for hourly labels. Ask UNH, Fraport, or DFS whether they can provide time-stamped operating-direction and runway-use data for research/hackathon use.

If labels must be inferred from flight tracks, document the method, missing-track handling, and uncertainty. Treat the result as an inferred label, not an authoritative DFS record. Avoid using a public forecast as the test-set truth; that would compare one forecast with another.
