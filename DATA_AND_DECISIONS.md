# Frankfurt operating direction: data and decisions

Research checked on 30 October 2026. Public access and a documented API are different things. The tables distinguish successful downloads, public displays and access that still needs permission.

## The decision for today's build

Use the recorded OD history in [data/ground_truth/dfld_hourly.csv](data/ground_truth/dfld_hourly.csv) for an initial evaluation against actual-operation proxies. It contains 24,546 stable hourly labels, backed by 49,584 half-hour records for local dates 2024-01-01 through 2026-10-29. The [download manifest](data/ground_truth/dfld_manifest.json) identifies every fetched monthly page. These labels come from DFLD's recorded operating-direction display, not from a weather rule. DFLD explicitly distinguishes real operating-direction distribution [reale Betriebsrichtungsverteilung] from the wind-derived decision criterion. [DFLD explanation](https://www.dfld.de/Mess/Language/StatWettHelpG.html), [example monthly history](https://www.dfld.de/Mess/StatWetter.php?R=1&D=01.01.2025).

Call this source **DFLD recorded OD, public proxy**. It is not a certified DFS log of every movement. DFLD says its OD information uses Internet sources, especially airport-operator websites. It does not document the exact within-bin sampling convention. [DFLD provenance](https://www.dfld.de/Mess/Language/MessHelpG.html).

Keep a separate **INFERRED** label family for METAR plus the challenge rules. That remains the fallback for missing OD observations and for runway 18 weather usability. Report accuracy against recorded OD separately from agreement with INFERRED labels. Otherwise a model can appear successful merely by reproducing the rule that created its target. This is a methodological recommendation based on the challenge's requirement to compare against the directions actually flown. [Official challenge brief](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md).

## 1. Who decides, and how

### The official challenge rules

The supplied brief is the authority for the hackathon baseline. It specifies the following rules; it does not identify a tower software product or define who formally signs a runway closure. [Official challenge brief](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md).

| Item | Rule in the supplied brief |
|---|---|
| Parallel runway direction | OD 25 toward 250 degrees true, OD 07 toward 070 degrees true. The parallel system changes together. |
| Choice of OD | Landings determine the direction; headwind is preferred. |
| Landing tailwind | At least 10 kt requires an immediate direction change. |
| Smaller tailwind | Below 10 kt may be accepted for up to 30 minutes. If it persists longer, switch to the headwind direction. |
| Runway 18 | Departures toward 180 degrees true only. At least 15 kt tailwind makes it unusable. It can operate with either parallel-runway OD. |
| Crosswind | More than 40 kt, including gusts, closes the runway. |

All entries above come from the [official challenge brief](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md). Boundary comparisons matter: 10 and 15 kt are already at the forbidden tailwind limits; the crosswind wording is strictly greater than 40 kt.

### What the airport sources establish

DFS sets the operating direction. Fraport identifies the responsible DFS shift management [verantwortliche Schichtleitung] and says it must consider actual upper winds and short- and medium-range DWD forecasts as well as surface conditions. This supports describing the decision owner as the DFS tower shift supervisor, rather than airport ground handling or the challenge sponsor. [Fraport operating direction](https://www.fraport.com/de/nachhaltigkeit/nachbarschaftsdialog/laerm-und-luft/flugbetrieb/betriebsrichtung.html).

Fraport manages airside resources in coordination with airport partners. Its apron control [Vorfeldkontrolle] and the DFS tower have distinct operational roles. Airport regulations require separate tower permission before entering the maneuvering area. Fraport also closes runways for maintenance. Therefore weather-based non-use of runway 18 and a formal infrastructure closure must not be treated as the same event. No reviewed source assigns OD authority to FraAlliance. [Fraport operations](https://www.fraport.com/de/geschaeftsfelder/betrieb/flug--und-terminalbetrieb.html), [airport regulations](https://www.fraport.com/content/dam/fraport-company/documents/geschaeftsfelder/service/richtlinien-und-zahlungsbedingungen/richtlinien/de/C2.1%20FBO%20Flughafenbenutzungsordnung%20v1.4.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original.media_file.download_attachment.file/C2.1%20FBO%20Flughafenbenutzungsordnung%20v1.4.pdf), [runway maintenance](https://www.fraport.com/de/nachhaltigkeit/nachbarschaftsdialog/laerm-und-luft/flugbetrieb/bahnensystem-und-betriebszeiten.html).

A DFS presentation says every direction change requires substantial coordination [hoher Koordinationsaufwand] and a stable expected wind situation. It identifies runway anemometers, calculated runway-parallel wind and probabilistic DWD forecasts, with pilot reports as qualitative evidence. It describes the certified quantitative information as restricted to DFS use [nur Nutzung durch die DFS]. This is a 2020 explanation of practice, not a replacement for the supplied 2026 challenge thresholds. [DFS Tower Frankfurt presentation, slides 6 and 10 to 12](https://www.flk-frankfurt.de/eigene_dateien/sitzungen/254._sitzung_am_19.2.2020/top_3a_-_praes._dfs__bestimmung_der_betriebsrichtung.pdf).

UNH puts the gap between the last movement in the old direction and the first in the new direction at about 3 to 8 minutes. It says the process from higher tailwind to the first landing in the new direction can take up to 30 minutes. Those are descriptions of transition time, not a guaranteed advance notice to ground handlers. [UNH operating-direction explanation](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/basiswissen/betriebsrichtung-und-rueckenwind/).

### Keep the noise preference separate from the baseline

The City of Frankfurt describes retaining OD 25 with up to 5 kt tailwind for noise reasons. Westbound operation is the preferred noise direction [lärmärmere Betriebsrichtung]. This differs from the challenge's 10 kt safety threshold and 30-minute tolerance. Do not silently change the baseline to 5 kt. Use the public noise preference as a possible explanation for differences between rule labels and recorded OD. [City of Frankfurt](https://frankfurt.de/themen/umwelt-und-gruen/umwelt-und-gruen-a-z/laerm/fluglaerm/betriebsrichtung), [Fraport](https://www.fraport.com/de/nachhaltigkeit/nachbarschaftsdialog/laerm-und-luft/flugbetrieb/betriebsrichtung.html).

## 2. The data behind the decision

| Input | What it measures or means | Public substitute and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Runway anemometers and touchdown-zone wind | Local runway conditions. DWD's local MET REPORT format supports runway, touchdown zone [Aufsetzzone], midpoint and end identifiers, two-minute mean wind, and gust information based on three-second means. | No keyless raw FRA runway-sensor feed was found. METAR and CDC station observations are substitutes with different spatial and averaging definitions. [DWD observing handbook, local-report section](https://www.dwd.de/DE/leistungen/lf_11_flugwetterbetriebsdienste/handbuch_flugwetterdienste_ops_4.0_mit_aenderungen.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2). |
| METAR | Airport weather observation, including wind and reported gusts. EDDF reports are usually twice hourly. | IEM EDDF download worked. METAR is not the same as the tower's runway-specific two-minute wind. UNH describes METAR wind as a ten-minute mean. [IEM](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml), [UNH](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/basiswissen/betriebsrichtung-und-rueckenwind/). |
| TAF | Issued airport forecast with validity periods, changes and temporary conditions. | IEM's TAF service is public, but the tested EDDF requests returned only a CSV header. Do not assume it provides EDDF history. [IEM TAF documentation](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cgi-bin/request/taf.py?help=). |
| DWD forecasts | ICON-D2 regional fields, ICON-EU European fields and specialized aviation products. | ICON GRIB files are keyless. The tested 00 UTC wind listings extended to 48 h for D2 and 120 h for EU. The pc_met briefing service requires paid access. [D2 wind files](https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/nwp/icon-d2/grib/00/u_10m/), [EU wind files](https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/nwp/icon-eu/grib/00/u_10m/), [pc_met](https://www.pcmet.de/). |
| Forecast tailwind | Project forecast wind onto each runway axis. Use `max(0, -speed * cos(direction_from - runway_heading))`, with angles converted to radians. | Compute this from public wind speed and direction. This is a derived feature, not a separate sensor. Keep the true-degree convention in the challenge brief and consistent wind units. [Official brief](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md). |
| Hysteresis | The rule depends on the existing direction and how long a tailwind has persisted. | A single hourly wind observation cannot recover the exact 30-minute history. Use the available subhourly sequence and retain uncertainty about unobserved intervals. The challenge defines the timer; the DFS presentation supports the practical need for stable conditions. [Official brief](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md), [DFS presentation](https://www.flk-frankfurt.de/eigene_dateien/sitzungen/254._sitzung_am_19.2.2020/top_3a_-_praes._dfs__bestimmung_der_betriebsrichtung.pdf). |
| Noise and other constraints | Noise preference, approach equipment and operational availability can affect the chosen direction. | Publish the 5 kt westbound preference as a separate real-world factor. UNH also mentions upper winds, pilot reports and ILS work as reasons for apparent disagreement with surface wind. [City of Frankfurt](https://frankfurt.de/themen/umwelt-und-gruen/umwelt-und-gruen-a-z/laerm/fluglaerm/betriebsrichtung), [UNH](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/basiswissen/betriebsrichtung-und-rueckenwind/). |

### Forecast-time discipline

Previous Runs aligns fields to fixed lead offsets: `previous_day1` is the forecast about 24 hours before valid time, and `previous_day2` about 48 hours before valid time. These do not provide distinct 1 h and 6 h forecast vintages. They can support a conservative older-forecast experiment, but its lead interpretation must be stated. [Previous Runs documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api).

Historical Forecast stitches successive runs. Use Single Runs when claiming a complete 48-hour forecast issued at one time. The `run` parameter denotes initialization, not public availability. Regional runs typically need another 1 to 3 hours to publish, and global runs 4 to 6 hours. Choose only a run available before the simulated issue time. This is necessary even when the initialization precedes that time. [Historical Forecast](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api), [Single Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api).

[Historical database](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/trino.html), [REST response fields](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/rest.html). |

The hourly extraction keeps a row only when both half-hour OD bins agree. It excludes 240 change hours and six ambiguous or nonexistent daylight-saving clock hours. The source clock is treated as Europe/Berlin. A current-switch comparison with UNH supports that choice; the January/July wind-alignment checks described in the data README support the winter/summer offset. This is evidence for the conversion, not an explicit timezone declaration by DFLD. All conversion assumptions, counts and exclusions are recorded in the [dataset README](data/ground_truth/README.md), with [DFLD October history](https://www.dfld.de/Mess/StatWetter.php?R=1&D=01.10.2026) and [UNH current status](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/anwendungen-service/aktuelle-betriebsrichtung-und-prognose/) as source checks.

Do not hide the excluded change hours. Report stable-hour accuracy and coverage, then inspect direction-change timing against the retained half-hour records. The dataset labels displayed configuration; it does not establish that a flight occurred in every hour, especially overnight. Exact jury alignment still needs the jury's timestamp convention and authoritative movement labels. This follows from the half-hour source resolution and the [challenge's flown-direction target](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md).

If track-derived validation becomes possible, use low-altitude final approach or initial departure segments near the runway. Classify eastward or westward runway-axis motion, exclude runway 18 departures from the OD vote, reject turns and airborne overflights, deduplicate aircraft and require enough independent movements. Preserve no-traffic and mixed-transition intervals as unknown. This is a proposed derivation, not a completed download. It needs archived positions, true track, altitude and timestamps, which OpenSky documents. [State-vector schema](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/trino.html).

The cost of DFLD is extraction and quality checks, with no account needed. The cost of archived ADS-B is access approval, track processing and incomplete near-ground coverage. METAR plus rules is the cheapest fallback but supplies **INFERRED** configuration, not observed flights. Runway 18 operational non-use also cannot prove wind-driven unavailability. [DFLD provenance](https://www.dfld.de/Mess/Language/MessHelpG.html), [OpenSky access](https://opensky-network.org/data), [official rules](frankfurt-airport-operating-direction-forecast.md).

- The certified runway-anemometer and calculated runway-parallel forecast feed used by DFS. No keyless raw feed was found; the DFS presentation marks the certified quantitative source for DFS use. [DFS presentation](https://www.flk-frankfurt.de/eigene_dateien/sitzungen/254._sitzung_am_19.2.2020/top_3a_-_praes._dfs__bestimmung_der_betriebsrichtung.pdf).
- An unrestricted historical feed from Fraport's operational CSA tool. The public description specifies a roles-and-rights system. [CSA description](https://cdm.frankfurt-airport.com/content/dam/fraport-company-cdm/documents/binary/documents/10th-customer-forum/01_a-cdm_forum-10_neues-csa-tool.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./01_a-cdm_forum-10_neues-csa-tool.pdf).

[UNH forecast](https://www.umwelthaus.org/fluglaerm/anwendungen-service/aktuelle-betriebsrichtung-und-prognose/), [TraVis](https://travisfra.topsonic.aero/travis.php?lang=de).
