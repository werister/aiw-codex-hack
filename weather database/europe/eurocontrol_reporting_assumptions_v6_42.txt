1   Introduction 

This document contains some specific reporting transformation rules or specific

filters applied in the PFR reporting (e.g. NM monthly Network Operations Report

(NOR), ATFM Statistics Dashboard, FATHOM). 

•   Section 1 contains the transformation rules mapping the operation

regulation causes of delay into new reporting group of causes of delay. 

•   Section 2 describes the computation of the ATFM delay and its

categorisation. 

•   Section 3 contains the list of published Statistical AUA in our en route

reporting, together with the list of excluded ones (for duplicate reason

for example) and “TMA” ones. 

•   Section 4 contains the CFMU AUA compositions of the published

Statistical AUA 

•   Section 5 contains the CFMU TVS compositions of the published

Statistical TVS 

•   Section 6 contains the history of the Summer Delay Forecast and Annual

Delay Optimum and Traffic Forecast for ACC entities. 

•   Section 7 contains the definition of the different axis flow used in online

dashboards and reporting. 

•   Section 8 contains the definition of the different geographical zones

used in the PRF reporting. An additional table shows the zones used in

some specific reports.

7   Traffic Axis Flow Definitions 

The table below shows the traffic flow definitions used in interactive reporting

dashboards (FATHOM, FLAIR). 

•   Intra-CTRY domestic: Flights that depart and arrive in the same country within

NM Area. 

•   Intra-NM: Flights that depart from one country within NM Area and arrive at a

different country within the NM Area. 

•   Out-of-Area: Flights that depart from one country with NM Area and arrive at a

country outside the NM Area or flight that arrive in a country within the NM

Area that have departed from a country outside the NM Area. 

•   Over-Flights: Flights that depart and arrive at countries outside of NM Area. 

Flow Axis are identified at a flight level depending on 3 criteria: 

•   first NM Area entry/NM ADEP 

•   Which ACC they pass through 

•   First NM Area exit/NM ADES 

Every flight is assigned to 1 of 19 possible options depending on the ACC they entered

within the NM area. 

Based on the above, within each Flow Axis there are Sub Flow Axis showing how the

flight moved within the flow axis.
