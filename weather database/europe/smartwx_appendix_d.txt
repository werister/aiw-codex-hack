Collaboration by using APOC is key 

Common Situation Awareness 

•   One common plan for the whole winter 

( Snowplan ) 

•   One common weather platform provide by our 

MET provider 

•   One dedicated Winter Coordinator who liaisons 

with 

•   APOC 

•   ATC (Local TWR) 

•   Snow Removal team 

•   Ground Handlers 

•   Dual weekly weather coordination meeting 

•   APOC 

•   ATC (Local TWR and Approach control) 

•   Met Provider 

Collaborative Decision Making 

•   In case a negative weather situation might occur 

(Heavy snowfall), APOC Supervisor calls for a 

Tactical Traffic Forum 

•   APOC 

•   ATC (Local TWR and Approach control) 

•   Met Provider 

•   Ground Handlers 

•   Airlines 

•   At this meeting all actors shares their demand and 

capacity, based on the balance/imbalance the forum 

creates a common plan to handle the expected 

capacity drop 

•   The plan is shared to all actors through the AOP 

Facilitated by a Winter Coordinator
