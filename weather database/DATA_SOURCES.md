# Additional data sources

Checked against primary portals on **30 October 2026**. No new bulk data were downloaded for this review. Access policies were checked; account approval, archive completeness and individual historical records were not tested. “Available” refers to the local research archive.

The most informative combination for understanding disruption is **flight/aircraft rotation → observed weather → traffic-management intervention → recovery**. Monthly aggregates cannot reconstruct that sequence. For the FRA Alliance objective, these sources provide supporting mechanisms; US effect sizes and reporting categories are not Frankfurt operating-direction or runway-availability labels.

| Priority and source | Empirical question | Time and resolution | Access, limitations and current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · BTS Reporting Carrier On-Time** | Which preceding aircraft delay propagates into the next segment, and how much does turnaround slack absorb? | Historical flight segments, aircraft tails, gate/runway times and reported causes. | [BTS](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/). **Available:** 2025 cause aggregates, analysis and reporting definitions; 2016–2024 aggregate acquisition underway. Full flight-level rotation data have not been acquired. Cause codes are administrative attribution, not an independent causal measurement. |
| **1 · IEM ASOS/AWOS/METAR** | What local visibility, gusts, thunderstorms or icing coincided with a disruption and recovery? | Historical and near-current station reports; routine, special and some five-minute reports. Separate US one-minute ASOS archive from 2000. | Public [CSV service](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml), scripted requests; up to 1,000 station-years per selected-station request. Little quality control. Precipitation field unavailable for non-US stations. **Not downloaded.** AWC EDDF weather samples are already available from a different provider. |
| **1 · FAA ATCSCC Advisories** | Which Ground Stop, Ground Delay Program or reroute was announced, revised or cancelled? | Historical advisories selectable by day; [current NAS status](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) separately available. | Public [archive](https://www.fly.faa.gov/adv/advAdvisoryForm). Archive start and completeness were not verified. Announced restrictions and expected delays are not realized flight outcomes; reconstruct revisions and cancellations. **Not downloaded.** |
| **2 · FAA ASPM** | Is time lost at the gate, on taxiways or in flight? How does demand/throughput relate to weather and delay? | Airport, city-pair and taxi reports; detailed airport products by hour/quarter-hour. Initial 55-airport history from January 2000, expanded later. | [Portal](https://www.aspm.faa.gov/), [documentation](https://aspmhelp.faa.gov/index/Aviation_System_Performance_Metrics_%28ASPM%29.html). Public airport/city-pair/taxi data within 60 days after month-end. Preliminary, individual-flight and other restricted reports require authorization. Revisions possible. **Identified through reviewed literature; raw data absent.** |
| **2 · FAA OPSNET** | Which ATC facility caused the delay, and where was it experienced? Weather, traffic volume, runway or equipment? | Historical facility/day reports; operations from fiscal 1989, delays from fiscal 1999. | [Overview](https://www.aspm.faa.gov/aspmhelp/index/Operations_Network_%28OPSNET%29.html), [definitions](https://aspmhelp.faa.gov/index/OPSNET__Delays.html). Public finalized data on day 20 of the following month; preliminary next-day access restricted. Reportable ATC detention of IFR traffic ≥15 minutes is **not BTS gate-arrival lateness**. System Impact and Occurred At are different perspectives. **Not downloaded.** |
| **2 · NASA ASRS** | How do weather, deicing, dispatch, communication, diversions and duty limits interact in real operational accounts? | Historical anonymized incident narratives and expert-coded fields; not live operations. | Public [search/export](https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/search/database.html); CSV/XLS/DOC, maximum 10,000 incident records per export. NASA does not verify the reported facts. Voluntary reporting supplies no representative denominator for prevalence. [Caveats](https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/overview/caveats.html). **Not downloaded.** |
| **2 · NOAA NCEI Global Hourly / ISD** | Are weather associations stable across seasons, years and stations? | Global hourly/synoptic station observations; station-specific coverage, earliest records in 1901; active stations updated daily. | Public [HTTPS/API products](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/integrated-surface-database). Documented quality control; gaps and variable availability still matter. IEM partly incorporates ISD, so these are not fully independent observations. **Identified in reviewed literature; raw data absent.** |
| **3 · NOAA Storm Events** | Which regional severe-weather episodes plausibly disrupted several airports? | Historical US event times, geography and narratives; begins 1950, broad event-type coverage from 1996, several months' publication lag. | Public [search/bulk CSV](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/) and [coverage explanation](https://www.weather.gov/unr/storm_reports). Event catalogue, not continuous airport weather. Spatial proximity alone does not establish flight impact. **Not downloaded.** |
| **3 · OpenSky** | What holdings, detours, approach sequences or throughput changes appear in actual trajectories? | Live and historical ADS-B/Mode-S observations; no pre-2013 data, uneven coverage. | [Data](https://opensky-network.org/data), [FAQ](https://opensky-network.org/about/faq), [API](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/rest.html). Full historical Trino access normally requires an eligible institution; private learning projects use curated releases. API limits apply. No native schedules, cancellation or delay labels. Missing reception is not a cancelled flight. **Not downloaded.** |

## Suggested use

For supporting US research, reconstruct one bounded disruption episode using flight records, local METAR/SPECI and the advisory timeline. Check whether delay continues after local weather improves. Examine aircraft rotations separately to distinguish propagation from local exposure. A case study establishes chronology; causal effect estimation additionally needs defensible comparison flights/days and controls for demand, season and time of day.

ASRS is particularly useful for learning mechanisms: code a small set of reports into trigger, operational response, bottleneck and consequence. For the actual FRA task, prioritize Frankfurt weather, verified runway configuration/availability observations and relevant European operational data. No additional US weather collection is proposed here.

## Reddit

Reddit can supply search terms, disruption dates and leads to original sources. Verify any operational claim against the linked FAA notice, weather observation and flight data. Comments and claimed professional roles do not establish cause or population frequency.

For structured learning from operational accounts, prioritize ASRS over Reddit because ASRS offers coded, exportable incident records—while retaining its explicit non-verification and selection-bias caveats. Reddit is a supplementary source-discovery and hypothesis-generation tool. This review did not evaluate specific Reddit incidents or audit Reddit access terms.
